Privacy Policy for ReelSave

Last updated: 10 September 2026

ReelSave (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a mobile app that downloads publicly available Instagram media to your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

  • No account, no login, no sign-up.
  • We collect nothing. No name, email, phone, contacts, location, or identifiers are collected by us.
  • no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No server stores your content or activity. Links you paste, media you download, and your download history stay only on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following is created and kept locally on your device and never sent to us:

DataWherePurpose
Download history (link, thumbnail URL, media type, timestamp)Local database on the deviceShow your past downloads on the History screen
Downloaded photos and videosYour device galleryThe core function of the app
Temporary files during a downloadApp cache (deleted right after saving)Intermediate download step

You can delete individual history entries, or clear all history, from the History screen at any time. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Data that leaves your device

To fetch a video, ReelSave loads the post’s public Instagram embed page in a hidden in-app browser on your device, then reads the media URL from it and downloads the file directly from Instagram’s servers. This happens entirely on your device, over your own connection, with no login. ReelSave has no backend server and sends your data to no one.

Standard network requests to Instagram’s servers are subject to Instagram’s / Meta’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Photos and media (read/write) — only to save downloaded files into your gallery. The app does not read or upload your existing photos.
  • Internet / network state — to load the public post and download the media.

Children

ReelSave is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live) and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not affiliated with Instagram or Meta

ReelSave is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Instagram, Meta Platforms, Inc., or any of their subsidiaries. “Instagram” and “Meta” are trademarks of Meta Platforms, Inc. ReelSave only accesses content that is already publicly available and only when you explicitly ask it to.
